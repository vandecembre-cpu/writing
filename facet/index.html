    Chapter 01 · Define

    What is style?

    Style is how you say who you are before you say anything at all.

    It lives in the way you put things together, what you reach for when no one is watching, and how you want to feel when you walk out the door. There is no correct answer and no one to impress but yourself.

    Trends tell you what everyone is wearing. Style is what you keep choosing, and why.

    Personal

    It belongs to your body, your budget, your climate and your days. Someone else's rules can inspire you. They can't define you.

    Ever-changing

    A new job, a new city, a new decade or a new mood will change what feels right. That is growth, not inconsistency.

    Faceted

    You are a colleague, a friend, a partner, a weekend wanderer. Each facet of you gets dressed a little differently.

    Chapter 03 · Your closet

    Shop your closet first

    Before you buy anything new, find the clothes that already make you look good and feel good. They are your blueprint.

    Pull everything out. Try it on. Ask four questions of every piece.

    One

    Do I look good?

    Check in the mirror, in daylight.

    Two

    Do I feel like myself?

    Or like I'm wearing a costume?

    Three

    Does it fit today?

    Your body now, not a past or future one.

    Four

    Worn in the last year?

    A full year covers every season.

    Four yeses: keep. Two or three: maybe. None or one: let it go. You can always override.

    Your blueprint

    The feel-good pieces

    Everything you kept that both looks good and feels like you.

    Think color, fabric, fit, silhouette, how they move.

    Chapter 04 · Let go

    The art of letting go

    Everything that didn't earn a keep gets a next home. Clearing space makes it easier to see what you love.

    Your let-go pile

    Choose where each piece goes.

    Where to sell

    Three places to list

    Depop

    Best for vintage, Y2K, streetwear and trend-led pieces. Style your photos and write a fun caption. You pack and ship.

    depop.com ↗

    Poshmark

    Good for mall brands, designer labels, shoes and bags. Buyers make offers, and you get a prepaid shipping label.

    poshmark.com ↗

    ThredUp

    The least effort. Order a Clean Out kit, fill it and mail it back. They photograph and price everything. Payouts are lower.

    thredup.com ↗

    The maybe box

    Put your maybes in a box and write today's date on it. Anything you haven't gone looking for in 30 days goes to the let-go pile.

    Donate

    Clean, wearable pieces. Check what local shelters actually need before you drop off.

    Swap

    Host a clothing swap with friends. Everyone leaves with something new.

    Tailor it

    Love it but the fit is off? A hem or a nip at the waist often beats replacing it.

    Recycle

    Stained or worn-through pieces go to a textile recycling drop-off, not the trash.
