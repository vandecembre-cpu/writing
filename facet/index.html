    How it works

    A workbook, not a quiz

    One step at a time

    Each step has a short read and an exercise. Skip around whenever you like.

    Your answers build on each other

    Your facets shape the closet check, which shapes your list, which shapes your budget.

    Private by default

    Everything saves in your browser as you go. Nothing is sent anywhere.

    The Edit

    Talk it through before you buy.

    Each piece on your list gets its own conversation: which facet it's for, what to spend, how it should feel. Then it points you to places to look.
